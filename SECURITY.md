# Reporting a weakness

Write to **jay@sgai.sg** (also listed at https://www.sgai.sg/.well-known/security.txt).

## In scope

- Forging a mark above its true assurance level (declared, observed, proven).
- Attaching a transcript to a mark it does not belong to.
- Removing a registered review session from a panel's denominator, or getting a session counted as an endorsement when the specification says it must be "none".
- Breaking the log: an undetected change, deletion or fork.
- Any vulnerability in the reference implementation.

## Already known

Section 11.3 of the specification lists the limits we accept: hidden instructions in assistants, bought activity among ordinary marks, injected marks, publisher-observed evidence, and others. Reports that restate these are welcome as ideas, not as vulnerabilities.

## Ground rules

Good-faith research that avoids other people's data, does not degrade the service and is reported privately first will not be pursued legally. Please do not flood the live log: it is append-only, and whatever you write stays.

Valid reports are credited by name, with consent, in the next version of the specification.
